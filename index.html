OUR STORY

From a Small Scoop
to a Big Dream

Gelatoo started with a simple idea — to bring happiness one scoop at a time. What began as a small passion project has grown into a brand loved by ice cream enthusiasts who believe in the power of good food and great moments.

We are inspired by people, places and flavors from around the world, and we craft every scoop with love, creativity and the finest ingredients.
